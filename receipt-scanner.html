Receipts

Receipt scanner app: scan receipts, keep the originals

In short: Scandora is a receipt scanner app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. You scan a receipt with your phone camera or an office network scanner, managed AI reads the vendor, the amount and the date, and the PDF can go to your own Google Drive or Trello account. The original stays on your device and in the storage you choose.

What should a receipt scanner app do?

Three things: capture a receipt before the thermal paper fades, read the numbers off it so you do not type them, and file it where your bookkeeping already lives. A receipt scanner that keeps every receipt locked inside its own app only moves the shoebox onto your phone.

How Scandora scans receipts

Hold your phone camera over the receipt and Scandora finds the edges of the page. A long till receipt or a multi-page invoice becomes one PDF with several pages. When a stack of paper is waiting on your desk, scan it on an office network scanner over eSCL/AirScan instead. Scanning itself works offline; the AI step and the upload need a connection.

What the AI reads off a receipt

Managed AI reads each scan without a template. On a receipt or an invoice it picks out the vendor, the amount, the date and, where they are printed, the invoice number and the IBAN. It also suggests a title and labels and recognises the document type, so a receipt is told apart from an invoice or a contract. Scandora keeps the title, the labels, the gross amount, the invoice number and the invoice date as the document's details, and each of them stays editable. Check the values before you file them: a creased or faded receipt can be misread.

Where your receipts end up

In accounts you already own. Each scan can become a PDF in your own Google Drive, named after its AI title, in a folder called Scandora or in a folder with a name you choose. With "Organize by document type" switched on, receipts land in a subfolder of their own. The same scan can instead become a Trello card with the PDF attached. Google Drive and Trello are the two destinations Scandora sends to today; the Google Drive page and the Trello page show each one step by step.

What leaves your phone, and where it goes

Your original receipts stay on your device and in the storage you choose. To read a receipt, managed AI sends the scanned page to Google Gemini, processed in the EU. While you are signed in, a low-resolution preview, the extracted text and the document's details sync to Scandora's servers in the EU. Scandora carries no ads. The EU servers page goes through every path one by one.

Finding a receipt again at tax time

Months later you rarely remember which folder a receipt went into. Ask Scandora a question about your documents, such as what you paid a supplier in March or when a warranty runs out, and you get an answer that cites the document it came from. Open that receipt and check the figure yourself.

Who it is for

Freelancers, small businesses and households that collect receipts for their bookkeeping or their tax return. Scandora runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, in English and German. The German page Belege scannen mit der App covers the same workflow for German-speaking readers.
